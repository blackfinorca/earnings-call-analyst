### Theme 1: The Stagflation Setup — Rising Oil Meets Sticky Inflation

**Theme Statement:** Oil's 9% single-session spike to $95/bbl is colliding with Core PCE inflation already at 3.10%, creating a stagflation risk that the market has not yet priced into defensive sectors.

**Thesis:** Stagflation — the toxic combination of rising inflation and falling growth — emerges when supply-side shocks (like energy price spikes) hit an economy that is already slowing. The current setup checks both boxes. GDP growth collapsed to 0.65% annualized in Q4 2025, yet Core PCE inflation is accelerating, not decelerating. Oil at $95/bbl feeds directly into transportation costs, manufacturing inputs, and consumer prices, making it nearly impossible for the Fed to cut rates even as growth stalls. This is the worst possible environment for cyclical stocks and one of the best for Energy and defensive sectors with pricing power.

**Evidence:**
- WTI crude oil surged 9.28% in a single session to $95.35/bbl, the highest level since mid-2023.
- Core PCE inflation rose to 3.10% in January 2026 from 3.00% in December, moving away from the Fed's 2% target.
- US GDP growth collapsed to 0.65% annualized in Q4 2025 from 4.38% in Q3, one of the sharpest decelerations outside a recession.
- ISM Manufacturing PMI decelerated to 51.6 from 52.4, signaling weakening industrial activity even before the oil spike fully filters through.

**Portfolio Implication:** Overweight Energy equities and avoid Consumer Discretionary and Industrials. Energy companies benefit directly from higher oil prices, while cyclicals face margin compression from rising input costs and weakening demand. Within defensives, favor Healthcare and Consumer Staples with demonstrated pricing power.
